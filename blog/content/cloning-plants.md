Lately, I have been propagating plants from cuttings.

“Cloning” sounds more dramatic than it is. Plant propagation uses material from an existing plant to grow another with the same genetic makeup. No science-fiction lab required.

The cutting has to move from relying on an established plant to growing its own roots. That takes time, and the visible part tells only part of the story.

It is a small, hands-on experiment: set things up, observe, and see how the plant responds.

I have not shared the species, setup, or results, so I will leave those details for another update. For now, plant propagation is one of the things I am working on away from the computer.
