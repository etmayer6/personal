I wanted a place for the parts of a project that do not fit on a portfolio card: experiments, wrong turns, small improvements, and what I learn along the way.

This notebook is for site updates, side-project progress, new things I learn, and the occasional note from outside the editor. There is no publishing schedule.

When I have something to share, I give Codex the facts and context. It can help shape a first-person draft and update the site, but the source stays plain Markdown.

The posts should sound like me: direct, specific, and honest about what is finished and what is still in progress. Codex can help with the blank page and formatting, but it should not invent details.

This is the first entry. The next can be about whatever comes along.
