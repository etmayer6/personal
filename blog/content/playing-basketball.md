Lately, I have been playing basketball.

It is a small update, but I want this notebook to include life away from projects, too.

Basketball asks for a different kind of attention than software work. Decisions happen quickly, and the next possession arrives before there is time to dwell on the last one.

The feedback is immediate: a pass connects or it does not; a shot drops or it misses.

I have not shared where or with whom I have been playing, so I will leave those details out. For now, basketball has been part of my time away from a screen.
