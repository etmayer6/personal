How it works

Each object plays its own repeating pattern. Drag across the garden to shift its start step; drag deeper or nearer to change its note. Notes stay in the same pentatonic scale.

1–5 choose objects, 6 chooses Move. Arrows select a space, Enter plants, Delete removes. Space plays or pauses, F goes fullscreen. Keyboard shortcuts work when the canvas has focus.
