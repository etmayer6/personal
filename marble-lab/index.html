Controls & mechanics

Choose a part, then click an empty space to place it. Click or drag a part to select or move it. Edit a part to reset the run.

Arrows move the cursor; Enter places/selects. A selects the ramp, V selects Move. Q/E rotates, D duplicates, Delete removes. Space runs/pauses, R resets, F goes fullscreen. Ctrl/Cmd Z undoes. Shortcuts work with the table focused.

Shift-drag pans; wheel or +/− zooms. Follow tracks an active marble. Switches open gates until reset. Portals need two parts with the same pair number. Dominoes tip at their pivot and knock neighboring dominoes.
