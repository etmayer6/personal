    03 / Architecture

    A short path from stimulus to proof.

    1. 01Scenario runnerLoads fixed setup and steps
    2. 02Simulated aircraftEmits fictional state
    3. 03Telemetry adapterNormalizes signals
    4. 04Rule engineEvaluates assertions
    5. 05Evidence reporterPackages the result

    The adapter is the seam: it keeps the rule engine independent from the shape of the simulated aircraft state.
